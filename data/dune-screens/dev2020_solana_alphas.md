## NOTE: Copy link

**1. _Each stats for each wallets on the ALL BOTS query is a cumulative stat calculated within 60 days range that wallet makes a trade_. that means the win rate, trust rate, average X, vol, Realized gains are all cumulated stats from each wallets in 60days of trades. For performance gains both the recent day activity filter and the min sol balance filter has now been hardcoded so this means it can longer be flexible/changed like before by any user running the dashboard. The default values are set to recent activity 2 days and min sol balance of 1 sol, so the dashboard will always show wallets that have at least made a trade within 2 days with a sol balance of 1 and above.**

**2. _Tokens that weren't bought by a wallet but shows sells aren't calculated as a win nor influence the **winrate** They only influence the **no of tokens transacted** and the **trust rate** of that user_**
